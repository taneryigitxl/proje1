# Third-party runtime

`vendor/babylon.js` and `vendor/babylonjs.loaders.min.js` are local copies of the official Babylon.js CDN distributions. They are committed locally so the game does not depend on a CDN at runtime. Babylon.js is licensed under Apache-2.0.

The current character, weapon, creatures, and environment are original procedural placeholder meshes; no third-party art assets are shipped.
